A short one this month — a few things catching our attention across the studio.

- **Variable fonts, used with restraint.** We're seeing more teams reach for variable type and immediately abuse the weight axis. The interesting work is happening in the subtle ranges — small optical-size shifts that most people will never consciously notice but absolutely feel.

- **Motion that respects the reader.** Scroll-driven animation is everywhere again. The good version guides attention. The bad version fights the content. The difference is almost always *restraint*.

- **The open web, quietly.** Personal sites, hand-built and idiosyncratic, are having a small renaissance. We're here for it.
